# Bibliography and Engineering Basis

This skill uses original procedural guidance informed by established software-engineering and visualization literature. It does not bundle book text.

## Software design and architecture

- Robert C. Martin — *Clean Architecture: A Craftsman's Guide to Software Structure and Design*.
- Martin Fowler — *UML Distilled: A Brief Guide to the Standard Object Modeling Language*.
- Eric Evans — *Domain-Driven Design: Tackling Complexity in the Heart of Software*.
- Vaughn Vernon — *Implementing Domain-Driven Design*.
- Simon Brown — *Software Architecture for Developers* and the C4 model body of work.
- Grady Booch, James Rumbaugh, Ivar Jacobson — *The Unified Modeling Language User Guide*.
- Craig Larman — *Applying UML and Patterns*.
- Martin Kleppmann — *Designing Data-Intensive Applications*.

## Visual communication and diagrams

- Edward R. Tufte — *The Visual Display of Quantitative Information*.
- Colin Ware — *Information Visualization: Perception for Design*.
- Scott McCloud — *Understanding Comics* (visual sequencing/communication concepts).
- Gene Kim et al. — *The DevOps Handbook* (flow and system work concepts).
- David J. Anderson — *Kanban*.
- Simon Wardley — Wardley Mapping body of work.

## Standards / official notation references to consult when exact semantics matter

- OMG Unified Modeling Language (UML) specification.
- OMG Systems Modeling Language (SysML) where systems-engineering requirements/structure require it.
- Mermaid official documentation.
- PlantUML official documentation.
- C4 Model official documentation.
- Structurizr DSL documentation.
- Graphviz DOT documentation.
- D2 documentation.

## Use policy

Use these works as conceptual foundations. Do not reproduce proprietary diagrams, long passages, tables, or book-specific examples. Generate new examples and concise summaries.
